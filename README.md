### About Me

I am a 19-year-old developer, physics enthusiast, and first-year Computer Science student (AI & ML specialization) who builds at the intersection of theoretical physics, mathematics, and modern AI engineering.

My work centers on bridging the gap between abstract mathematical frameworks and real computational systems — whether that means automating tensor calculus in General Relativity, or orchestrating multi-agent AI systems that interact directly with operating environments.

I don't wait for curriculum to catch up with curiosity. Most of what I build, I taught myself , from Christoffel symbols and geodesic equations to LangChain pipelines and deployed SaaS products driven purely by the need to understand things from the core, not just use them.

**Research Interests:**

**Technical Philosophy:**
I build from first principles before touching libraries. I'd rather struggle through nested index loops implementing Christoffel symbols manually than use a pre-built solver I don't fully understand. That approach is slower — but it means I actually know what's happening under the hood.

**What drives me:**
Physics showed me that the universe has a mathematical backbone. AI showed me that machines can reason. I want to build things that sit exactly at that intersection — tools that make the hardest mathematics accessible, automatable, and useful.
